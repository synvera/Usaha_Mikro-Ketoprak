Our Story

born from a deep craving for rich, savory street-style ketoprak, we realized one thing: young people deserve traditional comfort food in a clean, stylish spot. starting from a humble cart and endless tweaks to our family peanut sauce recipe, our journey began.

we set out to prove that authentic Indonesian flavors can seamlessly blend with modern lifestyle. every plate we serve is more than just a satisfying meal—it’s a story of warmth and togetherness made accessible to everyone without breaking the bank.

We started with a humble wooden cart, a stone pestle, and an uncompromising love for authentic street flavor. Back when a warm plate cost just a few hundred rupiah, our mission was simple: to serve genuine comfort food that brings people together every single day.

Est. 1980s

In an era where honest food was priced for everyone, our journey began with fresh tofu, vermicelli, and rich crushed peanuts prepared right on the street corner. Times have changed, but our commitment never wavered. Today, we proudly serve that same legendary recipe with modern hygiene and the exact classic taste you remember.
