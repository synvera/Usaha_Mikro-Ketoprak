Our Story

born from a deep craving for rich, savory street-style ketoprak, we realized one thing: young people deserve traditional comfort food in a clean, stylish spot. starting from a humble cart and endless tweaks to our family peanut sauce recipe, our journey began.

we set out to prove that authentic Indonesian flavors can seamlessly blend with modern lifestyle. every plate we serve is more than just a satisfying meal—it’s a story of warmth and togetherness made accessible to everyone without breaking the bank.

we are a collective of food enthusiasts united by a passion for authentic ketoprak and a vibrant hangout culture

we work for everyone for whom ketoprak is not just a quick meal, but a source of comfort and joy. our dedication to serving five-star taste at pocket-friendly prices inspires us every day
